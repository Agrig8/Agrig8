# Code of Conduct

innov8 is an open-source project for food security and agricultural trade
efficiency. We want participation to be welcoming, respectful, and safe for
developers, designers, mediators, and agricultural experts alike.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Giving and accepting feedback on code and designs in good faith.
- Protecting the privacy of users, traders, and contributors. Never post
  wallet addresses, personal data, secrets, or private keys in public spaces.
- Reporting security problems privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Sharing credentials, private keys, or exploit details in public issues.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or the CI pipeline.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions,
chat channels) and to any situation where a contributor represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the
maintainers through a private channel listed in the repository, or by opening
a GitHub issue that does **not** include sensitive details and asking for a
private follow-up. Reports are handled confidentially. Security vulnerabilities
must go through [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not public issues.

## Maintainer Response

- Reports are acknowledged within **3 business days**.
- Maintainers review the report, may contact the parties involved, and decide
  on an outcome.
- Possible outcomes include a private warning, a temporary restriction from
  project spaces, or a permanent ban, depending on severity and history.
- Reporters and affected parties are informed of the outcome where appropriate.
- Maintainers may recuse themselves from a case that involves them directly.
